# Reading the reports

!!! warning "Under testing"
    Report wording and the trust rating are under active testing. Every sentence is generated by fixed rules
    from the run's own numbers (no AI-generated text), but the rules themselves are still being refined.

Both v2 reports (forecast and climate scenarios) start with a plain-language section and end with a
collapsible **Technical details** section for specialists.

## Forecast report

| Section | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **The short version** | Expected total, busiest month, comparison with a typical year, which months look higher or lower than usual, and how many already-known months fell inside the likely range |
| **Expected cases, month by month** | A chart and table: expected cases, likely range (8 in 10 chance), comparison with a typical year and, where known, what actually happened |
| **How much can you trust this?** | A **Good / Moderate / Low** rating with its reasons |
| **Things to keep in mind** | Caveats that apply to this run |
| **Technical details** | Every model, every score, validation rules and the C-DSI v2 interpretation |

### The trust rating

One point each for: (1) the likely range contained the real number about as often as it should; (2) the
method shown beat the "same as recent years" baseline in past tests; (3) the methods broadly agree;
(4) the forecast looks no more than 12 months ahead. 4 points = Good, 2–3 = Moderate, 0–1 = Low.
"Not tested" means there was no past data to check against.

!!! note
    A "Good" rating means the method did well **in past tests on this dataset**. It is not a guarantee.

## Scenario report

Plain sentences per emissions pathway (cases a year, change from the baseline, likely range), a confidence
rating (backtest, climate-model agreement, sensitivity, and how clearly the data identify which climate
factors matter), and what the projections leave out. Decade tables, the backtest, the tree-model comparison
and methods are in the technical details.

## Words used

**Likely range**: where the real number should fall 8 times out of 10. **Typical year**: average for the
same month over the last five years. **Past tests / hindcasts**: forecasts made from earlier dates and checked
against what happened. **Ensemble**: the middle value of all models. **Emissions pathway (SSP)**: a standard
scenario for future greenhouse-gas emissions.
